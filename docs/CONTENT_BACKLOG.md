## How to use this

- **Build in order.** The list is sorted by value, not by hub. Take the next
  unbuilt row.
- **Standard is the live hubs.** Every article ships to the same bar: a short
  answer, a byline and dates, sourced statistics only (no unsourced numbers —
  the content validator blocks them), an FAQ block, and two-way hub/spoke
  linking. Titles here are the reader-facing headline (`h1`); write a short SEO
  `title` (≤ 37 chars before the brand suffix) at build time.
- **Do not build the cut topics.** The topic map marks 105 slugs as
  do-not-build (careers, niche/demographic playbooks, agency-building,
  competitor head-to-heads, geo-specific, speculation, thin listicles). They are
  out of scope; do not add them because they have search volume.
- **Wave 3 (192 topics)** lives in the topic map as a bare list. Pull from it
  only after Wave 2 is indexed and linked.
